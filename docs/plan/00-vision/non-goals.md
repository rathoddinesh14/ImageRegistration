# Non-Goals (for the foreseeable future)

- No generic `Dimension` / N-dimensional template in the first versions.
- No plugin system.
- No factories unless they are genuinely required.
- No dependency on VTK, ITK, OpenCV, Qt, or DICOM libraries in the core.
- No image I/O or visualization inside the core library.
- No premature parallelism or GPU support.
- No full 4D motion estimation until the spatial 2D/3D foundations are solid.
